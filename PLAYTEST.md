# First external playtest

Use https://echo-squad-after-hours.alkalibutterfly8.chatgpt.site on desktop. Test with three people who have not been told the rules. Do not coach them during the first run.

Record for each person: time to first spark, what they think the line does, what they think the triangle does, any pointer discomfort, whether they choose another run, and one sentence in their own words about what felt good or confusing. Ask before recording anyone. Do not invent names, quotes or outcomes.

The decision after these sessions: fix the most common misunderstanding or discomfort before adding features. Check that a visitor can start, finish, see a score, and hand the station to the next visitor. Try a separate spectator tab on the same browser and a cached reload without Wi-Fi before calling it booth-ready under sustained use.

No external sessions have been recorded yet. Automated game runs are documented in VERIFICATION.md and must not be counted as people.
