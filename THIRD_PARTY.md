# Third-party notices

Echo Heist uses Three.js 0.187.0 (MIT), https://threejs.org/ and https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js . Its unchanged ES-module builds and full license are saved in assets/vendor/. Reproduce them with `npm ci` followed by `npm run vendor`. package-lock.json pins the package and integrity hash. Three.js is served locally; no CDN is required at runtime.

All district geometry, textures drawn in code, character meshes, interface and synthesized sounds are original project code. No GTA/Rockstar models, maps, logos, music or textures are included.

The interface bundles unchanged Anton (The Anton Project Authors) and Bowlby One SC (Vernon Adams), from the Google Fonts repository, under their SIL Open Font Licenses. Font files and full notices are in assets/fonts/. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/anton and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bowlbyonesc . These are the actual fonts used; they are not Rockstar's custom fonts. The bold outlines and compact cash/radar presentation are an original interface inspired by the user's GTA reference.
